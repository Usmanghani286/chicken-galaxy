Chicken Galaxy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Chicken Galaxy ("the game") is an offline arcade game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the game does and does not collect.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers of our own. Your progress, coins, settings and high scores are stored only on your device, inside the game itself, and are never sent to us.

Advertising

The game may display ads provided by Google AdMob. If ads are shown, Google may collect and use device information (such as an advertising ID) to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads.

In-app purchases

If optional coin packs are offered, they are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details; Google handles the transaction under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

The game's Share button uses your device's standard share sheet. Anything you choose to share goes only to the app or person you pick.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect any data from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
